Two detectives. Two phones. One case.

A co-op noir mystery for two players on iPhone.

Each of you plays on your own iPhone, in a different room, seeing different clues. One of you finds the code, the other finds the lock. The only way through is to talk.

Level 1: The Grand Aurelia. A faded old hotel, a priceless gem, and a thief who leaves a calling card.

Level 2: The Velvet Ace. A bookkeeper has vanished inside a crooked casino, and the man who runs it never shows his face.
