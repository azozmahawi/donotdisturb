DO NOT DISTURB

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

The short version: Do Not Disturb does not collect, store, or sell any of your personal information. There are no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

This policy explains how the iPhone game Do Not Disturb: Case for Two (“the game”), made by Abdulaziz AlMuhawi (“I”, “me”), handles your information.

Information I collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-up, and no servers of its own. I don't receive your name, email, location, contacts, or any other personal information.

Playing online with Game Center

To play with a friend, the game uses Apple's Game Center. When you host or join a game, Game Center connects your device to your partner's device. During a game, the two devices send each other what's needed to play together: game progress, the detective each of you picked, and the detective name you typed in.

This information goes only to your partner, through Apple. I don't collect it or keep a copy. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Information saved on your device

The game saves small things on your own iPhone, such as which chapters you've reached and your settings. This stays on your device. It is never sent to me, and it is deleted if you delete the game.

Purchases

If the game ever offers something to buy, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never see your payment details.

Ads, analytics, and tracking

The game contains no ads, no analytics tools, and no third-party tracking, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13, because it collects no personal information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
